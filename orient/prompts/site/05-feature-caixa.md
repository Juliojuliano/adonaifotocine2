# Seção 4 — Feature 2: Caixa

## Estrutura visual

- **Tema:** Light
- **Layout:** Apple-style (eyebrow → headline → texto → imagem → 3 callouts)
- **Imagem:** Perfil lateral dramático (chiaroscuro) mostrando espessura + acabamento escovado + coroa

## Copy

```
[EYEBROW]
CONSTRUÇÃO

[HEADLINE]
Aço inoxidável escovado. 42mm. Sem concessões.

[LEDE]
A caixa é usinada em aço inoxidável e finalizada com escovação
satinada — o mesmo processo aplicado em relógios de mergulho
profissional. Resistente a arranhões do dia a dia. Hipoalergênico.
Projetado em 42mm: a proporção exata entre presença e elegância.

[IMAGEM — perfil lateral com chiaroscuro]

[CALLOUTS — grid de 3]
Callout 1: "316L" — "Aço inoxidável cirúrgico"
Callout 2: "Mineral" — "Cristal endurecido"
Callout 3: "42 × 11" — "Diâmetro × espessura (mm)"
```

## Prompt de imagem (Nano Banana 2)

**Referência:** anexe foto frontal do Orient.

```
Extreme macro photography of an Orient field watch viewed from the
RIGHT SIDE PROFILE at exactly 90 degrees, showing the case thickness
and brushed steel finish (refer to attached reference image for the
exact watch).

ANGLE: Pure side view at 90 degrees from the dial. The dial is NOT
visible — only the side profile of the case. The viewer sees:
- The full thickness of the case (showing the 11mm depth)
- The crown clearly visible in the center, protruding from the case
  (showing the knurled grip texture)
- The brushed steel finish on the case sides catching directional
  light (every micro-stripe of the brushing visible)
- The polished bevel edge between the brushed surface and the bezel
  (one mirror-finish line catching highlight)
- The curved lugs flowing into the strap top and bottom of frame
- The crystal visible as a thin curved line on the left edge
- The black silicone strap exiting the top and bottom of the frame,
  showing the perforations

WATCH (must match reference image exactly):
- Brushed stainless steel case ~42mm diameter, ~11mm thick
- Polished accent on the bezel edge
- Crown on the right at 3 o'clock position with knurled texture
- Domed mineral crystal slightly proud of the bezel
- Black silicone strap with visible perforations

LIGHTING — CHIAROSCURO:
- Single hard directional key light from upper-left at low angle
- Deep dramatic shadows falling on the case
- The light grazes the brushed surface, revealing every micro-stripe
- The polished bezel edge catches a single bright highlight
- Subtle shadow under the case showing depth
- Deep black background swallows most of the frame
- Only the illuminated edges of the case emerge from darkness
- Mood: technical, engineered, premium materiality — Caravaggio applied to product

COMPOSITION:
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 horizontal
- Watch case centered, filling 60% of the frame width
- Strap segments visible going up and down, soft focus
- Pure deep black background
- Tack-sharp focus on the case profile and crown
- Macro lens, shallow DOF
- Mood: "engineered solidity"

THE STORY: This image must communicate "this case was machined and
finished with intention. The brushed grain runs in one direction.
The polished edge catches light cleanly. 42mm of presence, 11mm of
substance."

DO NOT:
- Show the dial (this is a pure side profile)
- Tilt the watch (must be exactly 90 degrees)
- Make the case rose gold or yellow gold — it's brushed steel
- Use harsh studio lighting or distracting reflections
- Add elements not in the reference (no logos, no extra details)
```

## Nota especial

Esta imagem tem tratamento **chiaroscuro** proposital (Caravaggio/Rembrandt) — grande parte da cena em escuro absoluto, apenas os edges polidos e brushed emergindo da penumbra. Isso reforça o argumento "presença + engenharia" e cria uma foto que vira arte por si só.

A pequena "fagulha" vermelha do marker do mostrador aparecendo lá em cima é magnetismo visual — mesmo em foto lateral, o olho vai naquele único ponto de cor no frame.
