# Seção 5 — Feature 3: Resistência

## Estrutura visual

- **Tema:** Dark
- **Layout:** Apple-style (eyebrow → headline → texto → imagem → 3 callouts)
- **Imagem:** Macro extremo da coroa (knurling, threading, gasket sealed visíveis)

## Copy

```
[EYEBROW]
RESISTÊNCIA

[HEADLINE]
Water Resist 50m. Use na chuva. No mar. Na piscina.

[LEDE]
Certificação 5 ATM. Lave as mãos, tome banho, nade na piscina ou
enfrente uma tempestade sem pensar duas vezes. A vedação é testada
à pressão equivalente a 50 metros de profundidade. Você nunca mais
precisa tirar o relógio.

[IMAGEM — macro extremo da coroa]

[CALLOUTS — grid de 3]
Callout 1: "5 ATM" — "50m de resistência"
Callout 2: "Selada" — "Vedação em borracha"
Callout 3: "Rosqueada" — "Coroa de proteção"
```

## Prompt de imagem (Nano Banana 2)

**Referência:** anexe foto frontal do Orient.

```
Macro extreme closeup photography of the crown of an Orient field
watch (refer to attached reference image for exact watch design).

ANGLE: Ultra-tight macro shot focused on the crown area on the right
side of the watch case. The crown is at the center of the frame,
filling about 40% of the image. A portion of the case is visible
on the left, going slightly out of focus. The strap is barely
visible at the very edges, soft and abstract.

CROWN DETAIL (HERO of the image):
- Brushed stainless steel crown with knurled grip texture
  (vertical ridges) for screwing/unscrewing
- Threading visible where the crown meets the case (multiple thread
  turns showing the screw-down water resistance mechanism)
- Small Orient crown/logo stamped on the end face of the crown
- The crown is in its screwed-down (water-resistant) position
- Subtle micro-textures of the metal grain visible
- A visible RUBBER GASKET seal at the base of the crown, between
  crown and case (the technical component that makes water resistance
  actually work — this must be visible)

WATCH (must match reference image exactly):
- Brushed stainless steel case ~42mm
- White dial visible only as a soft blur on the far left edge, with
  hint of "3" numeral out of focus
- Black silicone strap visible only as soft texture at frame edges

LIGHTING:
- Single hard directional key light from upper-left at low angle
- The light reveals the metal texture — every micro-ridge of the
  knurling catches its own highlight
- Bright specular reflection on the polished edges of the crown
- Deep contrast: bright highlights on the polished edges, dark
  shadows in the recesses of the threading
- The threading catches light differently at each turn — depth visible
- Mood: technical, precise, engineered defense against water

COMPOSITION:
- Aspect ratio: 4:5 vertical OR 16:9 horizontal
- Crown at the optical center
- Extreme shallow DOF (f/2.0-2.8 macro feel)
- Tack-sharp focus on the crown's grip texture and threading
- The case curves out of focus rapidly
- Deep black background (chiaroscuro treatment — most of frame in shadow)
- Macro lens compression

THE STORY: This image must communicate "this watch was engineered
to keep water out." The visible threading, knurling, and gasket seal
are the visual proof of "5 ATM water resistance." You can SEE the
engineering that makes the claim true.

DO NOT:
- Show the full watch
- Make the crown smooth or featureless (the knurling MUST be visible)
- Make the case rose gold or yellow gold
- Add water droplets or moisture (keep it dry and technical)
- Use soft/diffused lighting that flattens the texture
```

## Insight de direção de arte

Esta imagem é onde storytelling visual atinge o auge. A coroa é o **ponto físico** onde a água entraria — e é exatamente esse componente que está em macro. O usuário lê "vedação certificada" e vê a peça mecânica que entrega a vedação.

Isso é pareamento copy-imagem nível campanha de relojoaria de luxo. A "fagulha" opcional de um número do mostrador borrado no canto (o "3" fora de foco) adiciona contexto sem competir com a coroa.
