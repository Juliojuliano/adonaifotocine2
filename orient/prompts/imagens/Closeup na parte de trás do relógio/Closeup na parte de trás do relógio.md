# Closeup na parte de trás do relógio

![image.png](Closeup%20na%20parte%20de%20tr%C3%A1s%20do%20rel%C3%B3gio/image.png)

Extreme macro closeup photography of the back of an Orient field
watch, showing the exhibition caseback with the mechanical movement
visible through the sapphire crystal.
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
COMPOSITION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

- The watch is photographed from directly behind, viewing the caseback
flat-on or at a very slight 5-10 degree angle
- The transparent exhibition caseback is the center of the image,
filling about 70% of the frame
- Surrounding the transparent crystal: the brushed stainless steel
caseback ring with engraved technical text
- A small portion of the strap is visible at the top and bottom of
the frame, exiting in soft focus
- The case sides are visible at the left and right edges, going
out of focus
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
THE MOVEMENT (visible through the exhibition caseback)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
A precision Japanese quartz/automatic movement is visible inside,
showing:
- The OSCILLATING WEIGHT (rotor) made of brushed steel with engraved
decorative pattern (perlage or côtes de Genève finishing)
- The "ORIENT" wordmark engraved on the rotor in small letters
- The MAIN BRIDGE with côtes de Genève (parallel wave pattern) finishing
- Multiple JEWELS (synthetic ruby bearings) visible — small red dots
embedded in the bridge, perfectly cylindrical
- BLUE STEEL SCREWS in regular positions, their heads slightly recessed
- The BALANCE WHEEL visible in one section (a fine wheel with
hairspring), if applicable
- Small ENGRAVED TEXT on the rotor or bridges showing technical
specifications: "21,600 vph", "21 jewels", "Made in Japan"
- The texture is OBSESSIVELY DETAILED — every screw thread, every
jewel facet, every brushed line on the metal visible
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
THE OUTER CASEBACK RING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
The brushed stainless steel ring around the transparent crystal
shows engraved text in small precise letters:
- "ORIENT" wordmark at the top
- "WATER RESIST 50M" engraved on one side
- "STAINLESS STEEL" or "JAPAN MOVEMENT" engraved on the other
- Serial number engraved at the bottom
- The brushed grain of the steel visible, catching directional light
- A polished bevel where the caseback ring meets the case body
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
LIGHTING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
- Soft directional key light from upper-left, warm color temperature
- The light reveals every micro-detail of the movement:
    - Côtes de Genève pattern catches highlights in waves
    - Jewels glow with deep red color
    - Blue screws show their cool blue tone in contrast
    - Brushed steel ring shows its grain
- Subtle reflection on the sapphire crystal of the caseback (just
enough to suggest it's under glass, but not obscuring details)
- Deep shadows in the recesses of the movement adding dimensionality
- Mood: technical, precise, museum-grade documentation
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
FOCUS & DOF
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
- Tack-sharp focus on the center of the movement (the rotor and
main bridges)
- Slight focus falloff toward the outer caseback ring (still readable)
- Macro lens compression (90mm or 100mm equivalent)
- Aperture feel: f/4-5.6 (deep enough to keep movement details sharp,
shallow enough to soften the strap edges)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
AESTHETIC & STYLE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
- Aspect ratio: 1:1 SQUARE OR 4:5 vertical
- Pure black background (deep, infinite blackness around the watch)
- References: Hodinkee macro photography, A Collected Man caseback
shots, Phillips Watches catalog photography
- Color grade: warm but technical, slightly desaturated, premium
watchmaking documentation aesthetic
- Subtle film grain for organic quality
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
ABSOLUTE RULES — DO NOT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
- DO NOT make the case rose gold, yellow gold, or any color other
than brushed stainless steel
- DO NOT use harsh studio lighting or visible reflections that
obscure the movement
- DO NOT add fictional brand names — use ORIENT consistently
- DO NOT make the rotor solid (it should show finishing patterns
and engravings)
- DO NOT show the dial side of the watch — this is a CASEBACK shot
- DO NOT use blurry or low-detail movement rendering — every screw,
jewel, and bridge must be sharp and identifiable
THE STORY THIS IMAGE TELLS:
"Inside this watch, Japanese precision engineering at work.
Every component visible, every detail considered, every screw and
jewel in its place. This is what '±20 seconds per month' looks like
under glass."
