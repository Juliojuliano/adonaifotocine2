COMPOSITION — CRITICAL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

- The watch fills approximately 55-60% of the frame, positioned
slightly off-center (about 40% from left edge, 50% vertically)
- Wrist is rotated at a natural ~15 degree angle, showing the
watch face fully readable but not flat-on (subtle perspective
giving the case dimensional presence)
- The watch is photographed from above the wrist, looking down
at a slight angle (~30 degrees from horizontal)
- Crown side (right side of the watch) angled slightly toward
camera, catching light on the brushed steel
- The strap exits the bottom-left corner of the frame, going under
the cuff of a knit sweater
- Top of the frame shows wrist skin and a hint of the back of the
hand, gently out of focus
- Aspect ratio: 4:5 vertical (Instagram portrait)

ABSOLUTE RULES — DO NOT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
- DO NOT change the watch design — match reference exactly
- DO NOT use roman numerals or stick markers
- DO NOT make the dial silver, blue, black, or any color other than white
- DO NOT add subdials, complications, or moonphase
- DO NOT make the case rose gold or yellow gold — it's brushed steel
- DO NOT make the strap leather, mesh, alligator, or anything other
than perforated black silicone
- DO NOT show the watch flat-on (front view) — it must have natural
wrist angle perspective
- DO NOT crop the watch in half or out of frame
- DO NOT show fingers, knuckles, or full hand
- DO NOT use harsh studio lighting or visible reflections in the crystal
- DO NOT add tattoos, bracelets, rings
The watch is the absolute hero. The wrist is the supporting character.
The light makes them sing together.
