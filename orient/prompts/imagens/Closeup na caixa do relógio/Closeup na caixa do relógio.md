Product photography of an Orient field watch (refer to attached
reference image for exact watch design).
ANGLE: Three-quarter perspective from above at approximately 45 degrees.
The watch is tilted toward the camera so that the dial is fully visible
and readable, but at a clear angle (not flat-on frontal). The case
sides are visible on the right, showing the brushed steel thickness
and the crown at 3 o'clock position. A small portion of both strap
segments exits the top and bottom of the frame.
WATCH (must match reference image exactly):

- Pure white matte dial with bold black ARABIC NUMERALS 1 through 12
- Inner gray 24-hour numerals (13-24) inside the main numerals
- "ORIENT" wordmark in black below the 12 position
- "Water Resist 50m" text above the 6 position
- Date window at 3 position with black numeral on white
- Small RED square markers at 12, 3, 6, 9 positions
- Red inverted triangle at top of outer ring
- Hour and minute hands: silver outline filled with red
- Red second hand with triangle counterweight
- Brushed stainless steel case ~42mm
- Black silicone strap with perforations
LIGHTING:
- Studio lighting, soft warm key light from upper-left
- The dial reads bright and luminous
- Subtle highlight catches the edge of the bezel
- The crystal shows just the slightest reflection without
obscuring readability
COMPOSITION:
- Watch fills 70% of the frame, centered
- Aspect ratio: 16:10
- Deep black background
- Tack-sharp focus on the dial — every numeral readable
- Shallow DOF — strap edges softening at the frame border
- Macro detail quality, professional luxury watch photography
DO NOT:
- Use roman numerals or stick markers
- Show the watch frontal (must be at angle)
- Add subdials, complications, or change the design
- Make the strap leather, mesh, or anything other than perforated silicone
