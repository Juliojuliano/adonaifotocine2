═══════════════════════════════════════════════
ANIMATION — CRITICAL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
The box starts CLOSED, exactly as shown in the first frame, perfectly
frontal to the camera. The animation has THREE distinct phases:
PHASE 1 (0–1.5s): STILLNESS
The closed box sits motionless. Subtle ambient atmosphere only.
Nothing moves. Builds anticipation.
PHASE 2 (1.5–4s): LID OPENS
The hinged lid slowly rotates BACKWARDS on its rear hinge, opening
smoothly upward and away from the camera. The lid pivots ONLY on
its rear edge — like a real clamshell box. The base of the box
remains COMPLETELY STATIONARY throughout. As the lid opens:

- The interior of the box is gradually revealed
- A soft warm glow emerges from inside as the lid lifts
- The lid finishes its rotation when it is approximately 110-120
degrees open (resting back, fully revealing the contents)
PHASE 3 (4–6s): REVEAL
The box is now fully open. The watch sitting inside is revealed
in its full glory:
- An Orient field watch (refer to attached watch reference image)
- The watch is centered inside the box, dial facing the camera
- White dial with arabic numerals visible, red accents
- Brushed steel case
- Black perforated silicone strap arranged neatly inside
- The watch is illuminated by the warm light spilling from above
- A subtle catch of light on the brushed steel case
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
CAMERA — ABSOLUTELY LOCKED OFF
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
- The camera does NOT move. No pan, no zoom, no dolly, no rotation.
- The camera position is identical from frame 1 to the last frame
- The box does NOT rotate, does NOT shift position
- ONLY the lid moves (rotating open on its rear hinge)
- ONLY the watch is revealed inside (it does not move, just becomes
visible as the lid opens)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
LIGHTING — DYNAMIC
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
- The ambient lighting on the closed box (soft warm key light from
upper-left) remains constant throughout
- As the lid opens, an ADDITIONAL soft warm glow emerges from inside
the box — as if the watch interior has its own subtle illumination
- This glow grows progressively as the lid opens further
- The glow is gentle, NOT a dramatic spotlight or flash
- The watch face catches the warm light naturally as it's revealed
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
ENVIRONMENT — STATIC
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
- Pure black background, completely unchanged throughout
- No flickering, no atmospheric shifts, no fog, no particles
- The black void around the box is constant
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
PACE & MOOD
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
- Slow, deliberate, premium pacing
- Constant velocity throughout the lid's rotation — no acceleration,
no deceleration, mechanical precision
- Mood: ceremonial, luxurious, "the unveiling of something special"
- Cinematic 24fps with smooth motion blur on the moving lid
- Reference style: Apple product reveal, luxury watch unboxing
on Hodinkee video, Tag Heuer product film
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
DURATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
6 seconds total. No music or sound effects.
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
ABSOLUTE RULES — DO NOT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
- DO NOT rotate the box or change its angle at any point
- DO NOT move the camera in any way
- DO NOT add hands, fingers, or any human element
- DO NOT change the lighting dramatically — only the interior glow
emerges gradually
- DO NOT add particles, smoke, sparkles, or magical effects
- DO NOT change the box position or have it slide around
- DO NOT show the watch moving inside the box — it's stationary,
only revealed by the opening lid
- DO NOT change the watch design — it must match the reference
- DO NOT add light flares, lens effects, or color shifts
- DO NOT use a colored background — must remain pure black
- DO NOT have the lid open in fewer than 2 seconds — must be slow
- DO NOT have the lid open more than 130 degrees — natural stop point
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
WATCH IN THE REVEAL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
When the box opens, the watch visible inside must match the
attached reference image exactly:
- White matte dial
- Black arabic numerals 1-12
- Inner gray 24-hour numerals
- "ORIENT" wordmark below 12
- "Water Resist 50m" above 6
- Red accent markers at 12, 3, 6, 9
- Red triangle at top of outer ring
- Silver hands with red fill
- Brushed stainless steel case
- Black perforated silicone strap arranged neatly in the box
